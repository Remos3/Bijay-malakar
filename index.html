Key Development Works

Health & Mega Infra

Ramkrishna Nagar mein naye Medical College & Hospital ka nirman aur Sribhumi Civil Hospital mein aadhunik suvidhayein.

Roads & Connectivity

Kamlatilla (₹96 lakh), Mukhya Mantrir Pakipath (₹75 lakh), aur MGNREGA ke tahat kai nayi sadko ka nirman.

Education Development

Dhupanpur Tribal HS School (₹4.5 crore), Pallishree HS mein Science Lab, aur Netaji Nagar mein Model School ka nirman.

Community & Anganwadi

Sannyasibari, Mongalpur, aur Gobindapur mein naye Community Halls aur modern Model Anganwadi Centres.

Public Welfare Schemes

CMAAA ke tahat 829 beneficiaries, ASHA workers, Anganwadi workers aur Mid-Day Meal cooks ko financial assistance.

Culture & Heritage

Ramkrishna Nagar mein Atal Bihari Vajpayee ji aur Anipur Trimukha mein Bir Lachit Barphukan ki murti ka anavaran.
