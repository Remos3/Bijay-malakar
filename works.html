Detailed Development Works

Health & Major Infrastructure

  • Ongoing construction of Medical College and Hospital at Ramkrishna Nagar (progress reviews conducted with ministers).
  • Indoor stadium complex (under construction) near the upcoming medical college.
  • Inspection and related work at Karimganj Civil Hospital, Sribhumi.
  • Inspection of New Co-District Office (CDC) construction at Ramkrishna Nagar.

Roads & Connectivity

  • Road at Kamlatilla, Ward No. 9, Ramkrishna Nagar town (₹96 lakh).
  • Road from Puspapriya ME School PWD road (near Narendra Sangha Club) to Kalinagar Pt-II under Mukhya Mantrir Pakipath Nirman Achoni (₹75 lakh).
  • Multiple roads under MGNREGA in Gandhinagar GP (Tengargul Ward No. 4 – ₹15 lakh; Chameli Ward No. 1 – ₹10 lakh; Chameli Bazar Ward No. 1 – ₹15 lakh).
  • Other local road connectivity improvements in the constituency.

Education

  • Integrated Science Laboratory at Pallishree High School, Baruala GP (inaugurated).
  • Model School Building foundation at Netaji Nagar High School.

Community Halls & Anganwadi Centres

  • Community Hall at Sannyasibari, Madhya Patiala Sanskrit Village, Patiala GP (under MLA-ADS 2025–26).
  • 74 No. Gobindapur Model Anganwadi Centre + Community Hall near Gobindapur Durga Mandap (under MLA-ADS 2024–25).
  • Community Hall at Narayan Mandir, Mongalpur Village (under PMAGY – ₹15 lakh).
  • Multiple Model Anganwadi Centres in Lalchara GP (foundation stones laid).

Welfare & Other Works

  • Distribution of financial assistance and certificates to ASHA workers, Anganwadi workers, and Mid-Day Meal cooks under Seva Sankalp Abhiyan.
  • Unveiling of full-length statue of Atal Bihari Vajpayee at Ramkrishna Nagar Block Road.
  • Unveiling of statue of Bir Lachit Barphukan at Anipur Trimukha.
