About Bijoy Malakar

Bijoy Malakar is an Indian politician from Assam and a dedicated member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). He is the elected Member of the Assam Legislative Assembly from the Ram Krishna Nagar (SC) constituency in Sribhumi district. Known for his simple lifestyle, grassroots connection, and commitment to local development, Bijoy Malakar works closely with the people.

Political Journey

2019 — First Election Victory

Won the Ratabari Assembly by-election on a BJP ticket.

2021 — Re-elected from Ratabari

Re-elected from the Ratabari (SC) constituency in the 2021 Assam Legislative Assembly election.

2023 — Constituency Delimitation

Ratabari was redrawn and renamed Ram Krishna Nagar. Bijoy Malakar continued to represent the people.

2026 — Historic Victory from Ram Krishna Nagar

Won from Ram Krishna Nagar (SC) with 1,34,013 votes, defeating his nearest rival by a margin of 83,765 votes.
