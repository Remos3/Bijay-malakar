About Bijoy Malakar

Bijoy Malakar is an Indian politician from Assam and a dedicated member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). He is the elected Member of the Assam Legislative Assembly from the Ram Krishna Nagar (SC) constituency in Sribhumi district. Known for his simple lifestyle, grassroots connection, and commitment to local development.

Political Journey

2019 — First Election Victory

He began his legislative career in 2019, winning the Ratabari Assembly by-election on a BJP ticket. His victory marked the beginning of his journey as a public representative.

2021 — Re-elected from Ratabari

In the 2021 Assam Legislative Assembly election, he was re-elected from the Ratabari (SC) constituency, reflecting the trust and support of the people.

2023 — Constituency Delimitation

Following Assam's 2023 delimitation process, Ratabari was redrawn and renamed Ram Krishna Nagar. Bijoy Malakar continued to represent the people.

2026 — Historic Victory from Ram Krishna Nagar

Won from Ram Krishna Nagar (SC) with 1,34,013 votes, defeating his nearest rival by a margin of 83,765 votes.
